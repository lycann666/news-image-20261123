THE WORLD TODAY — a one-image front page summing up Monday, 23 November 2026.
Lead story: the COP31 climate talks collapse in Antalya after the final text
drops all mention of fossil fuels. Second story: the Gulf war widens — Iran
strikes US bases in Bahrain and two more tankers are hit in the Strait of
Hormuz. Bottom strip: 14 missing after a drone hits Kyiv-region flats;
9 killed in 48 hours as the Gaza truce frays; BepiColombo enters orbit
around Mercury after eight years; and a factory fire in Indonesia leaves
24 dead and dozens missing.

The image is drawn by a pure Python standard-library program (zlib + struct
PNG writer, hand-built 5x7 bitmap font). No network, no randomness, no
clock: two runs write byte-identical PNGs.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, well under 1 MiB).
